# Refund policy

## Rules

1. Refunds are available for **30 days** from the delivery date.
2. Damaged or faulty items are always refunded in full, including postage.
3. Change-of-mind returns are refunded minus postage.
4. Personalised items cannot be returned unless faulty.
5. Sale items follow the same rules as full-price items.

## Notes

If the order was paid partly with a gift card, refund the gift card portion to
a new gift card and the rest to the original payment method.
